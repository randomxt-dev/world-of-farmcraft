The timeline

You start as a plain farmer with a shovel, 8 Silverleaf seeds and 2 silver on day 1. Ma Stonefield, Innkeeper Farley and Brog Hamfist hand out the quests that unlock everything else.

☀️The feel and the day
  • No fail state. Nothing dies, nothing is lost. A missed day only slows a crop.
  • Every day has a small plan: water, harvest, ship, then one bigger job.
  • There is always a next quest with a clear reward, so the next goal is never a mystery.
  • The world is the reward: quests send you to Goldshire, Fargodeep, the lake, Eastvale.

⛏️Mining and the smith

Fargodeep Mine, south-west of the farm. Copper veins refill each morning Built.

  • Floors 1–10: copper and tin. Floors 11–20: iron and silver; kobolds scared off with a lantern, not fought.
  • Floors 21–30: gold and gems (Malachite, Tigerseye, Shadowgem). A lift every 5th floor.

📜The Stonefield Restoration ledger

Ma's ledger has 6 pages. Each page is a bundle; finishing one fixes something on the farm or in Elwynn.

PageBundleReward

A full ledger (end of year 1) brings back the Stonefields' harvest festival: the "you did it" moment. After that the game is open-ended.
