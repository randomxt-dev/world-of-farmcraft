# World of Farmcraft: game design

A cozy farming game in the Stardew Valley mould, set on Stonefield Farm. This is the target; what
is built today is marked **(built)**. Prices are in copper (100c = 1s, 100s = 1g), as the bag and
the merchant window show them.

## The feel

- **No fail state.** Nothing dies and nothing is lost. A missed day only slows a crop down.
- **Every day has a small plan.** Water, harvest, ship, then pick one bigger job: chop, mine,
  build, fish, or visit town.
- **Every week has a visible goal.** It is always clear what the next upgrade costs and what it
  unlocks. The farm should look different at the end of each season.
- **The world is the reward.** Upgrades open up more of Elwynn: Goldshire, Fargodeep, the lake,
  Eastvale. Each place has a reason to go back.

## The day

| Time | |
|---|---|
| 06:00 | Wake in the farmhouse. Ma Stonefield's tip of the day; the weather for the day **(built)** |
| Morning | Water and harvest. Drop goods in the Shipping Crate **(built)** |
| Afternoon | One bigger job: chop **(built)**, mine **(built)**, build fences **(built)**, gather wild herbs **(built)**, shop in Goldshire **(built)** |
| Evening | Tend the animals **(built)**; cook, craft, talk |
| 02:00 | Pass out wherever you are and lose a little gold; sleeping in bed is free **(built)** |
| Overnight | Crops grow, the crate pays out, trees regrow **(built)** |

A day lasts about 17 real minutes **(built)**. A season is 28 days and a year is 4 seasons.

## Land: how many plots

| Stage | Plots | How |
|---|---|---|
| Start | 24 (4 rows × 6) on the leaf rows | Given **(built)** |
| Spring | Up to 48 | Dig more with Till Soil, anywhere on the field's furrows **(built, no cap yet)** |
| Copper Hoe | Till 3 in a row | Blacksmith upgrade |
| Field deed, 25s | The east field opens: up to 96 plots | Bought from Ma Stonefield |
| Sprinklers | Water 4 or 8 plots each morning | Crafted from copper and tin bars |
| Greenhouse, 2g | Grows all year | The barn's second floor, restored with wood and gold |

Digging is free, but each plot is one more thing to water. Watering, not land, is the early limit,
until sprinklers arrive. That is what makes the upgrades feel good.

## Crops by season

| Season | Crops |
|---|---|
| Spring | Silverleaf, Peacebloom, Earthroot **(built)**, Wheat |
| Summer | Mageroyal, Briarthorn **(built)**, Corn, Watermelon **(built)** |
| Autumn | Pumpkin **(built)**, Fadeleaf, Wild Steelbloom |
| Winter | Nothing outdoors. Greenhouse, mining, crafting, animals |

Today every crop grows all year. Seasons switch on after the greenhouse exists, so winter never
feels empty. Herbs bought as seeds stay cheap. The rare ones (Kingsblood, Liferoot) only come from
seeds found while gathering wild herbs and mining.

## Money

| Selling | Buying |
|---|---|
| Shipping Crate: full price, paid overnight **(built)** | Auntie Bernice at the farm: starter seeds **(built)** |
| Brog Hamfist, Goldshire inn: paid now, 80% **(built)** | Brog Hamfist: all seeds **(built)** |
| Weekly orders on the Goldshire board: a bonus for a set basket | Smith Argus, Goldshire: tool upgrades, bars |
| Special orders from townsfolk, which also earn friendship | Ma Stonefield: land, buildings, animals |

Rough earnings (crops sell well enough to pay for the next step):

- Week 1: about 1s a day, from Silverleaf and Peacebloom.
- End of spring: 10–20s a day, from Mageroyal, pumpkins and animal goods.
- Year 2: 1g or more a day.

## Tools and upgrades

Every tool is a button on the hotbar **(built: shovel, watering can, axe, fences, horse)**.

| Tool | Copper (Smith Argus) | Iron | Gold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Watering Can | Holds 20, waters 3 in a line | Waters 5 | Waters a 3×3 |
| Shovel | Tills 3 in a row | 5 | 3×3 |
| Axe | Fells in 2 swings | 1 swing; can clear stumps and logs | Splits Heartwood |
| Pickaxe | Copper and tin | Iron and silver | Gold, mithril, gems |

Each upgrade costs gold plus bars, and the tool is away at the smith for one day, as in Stardew.
Bars come from Fargodeep ore, smelted in a furnace you build by the barn.

## Fences and animals

- **Fences** **(built)**: draw a line with Build Fence. Each 4 yd section of Elwynn split rail
  costs 2 Simple Wood. Without enough wood, the rest is marked with posts to build later.
- **Gates**: a fence section that opens. Animals stay inside a closed pen.
- **Buying animals** from Ma Stonefield, once you have a coop or a barn:

| Animal | Price | Needs | Gives |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken | 8s | Coop | Small Egg daily **(built)** |
| Sheep | 15s | Barn | Wool every 3 days **(built daily)** |
| Cow | 20s | Barn | Ice Cold Milk daily **(built)** |
| Pig | 25s | Barn | Truffles in summer and autumn, found by the pig |

- **Care**: pet it each day and keep it fed (hay from tall grass, cut with the shovel). Friendship
  grows hearts. More hearts give better goods: Large Egg, Gold Milk, Fine Wool.
- **Breeding**: two adults of the same kind with 5 hearts, and a free space in the barn. A baby
  arrives in 7 days and grows up in 7 more. You name it.
- **Selling animals** to Ma Stonefield: half the buy price, plus a bonus for hearts. Show winners
  at the Goldshire fair also sell well.
- **Coop and barn upgrades** add space: 4, then 8, then 12 animals.

## Mining

Fargodeep Mine, south-west of the farm **(built: copper veins that refill each morning)**.

- Floors 1–10 have copper and tin.
- Floors 11–20 add iron and silver. Kobolds are scared off with a lantern, not fought.
- Floors 21–30 add gold and gems: Malachite, Tigerseye, Shadowgem.
- Every 5th floor has a lift, so you can start there next time.
- Gems go to the Goldshire jeweller, or into furniture and gifts.

## Getting around

| Step | Cost | |
|---|---|---|
| On foot | Free | 7 yd/s |
| Farm Horse | 50s at the Eastvale stables | 14 yd/s **(built, free for now: hotbar slot 5)** |
| Saddlebags | 20s | 4 more bag slots while riding |
| Swift Brown Steed | 5g | Faster; carries a full harvest to Brog in one trip |

Goldshire is about 600 yd from the farm: 40 s on the horse instead of 85 s on foot. Today the
horse is given at the start so you can try it. In the full game it is the first big purchase,
around the end of spring.

## The house

| Stage | Cost | |
|---|---|---|
| Stonefield farmhouse | Start | A bed **(built)**, a fireplace, one room |
| Kitchen | 10s + 50 wood | Cook: buff foods (more energy, faster growth for a day) |
| Second floor | 1g + 150 wood | Bigger bed, a chest, room for furniture |
| Cellar | 3g | Barrels to age cheese and mead, which sell for more |

Furniture is sold in Goldshire and Stormwind, and placed with a build tool like the fence tool.

## Goals (the "community center")

**The Stonefield Restoration**: Ma Stonefield's ledger has 6 pages, and each page asks for a
bundle. Finishing a page fixes something on the farm or in Elwynn:

| Page | Bundle | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| The Field | 5 of each spring crop | Sprinklers recipe |
| The Pen | Egg, Milk, Wool | Free barn upgrade |
| The Mine | 20 copper bars, 5 tin | The mine lift |
| The Woods | 100 wood, 10 Heartwood | The bridge to Eastvale reopens |
| The Town | 5 gifts to Goldshire folk | Goldshire Harvest Fair each autumn |
| The Lake | 5 fish from Crystal Lake | The fishing rod upgrade |

A finished ledger (around the end of year 1) brings back the Stonefields' old harvest festival.
That is the "you did it" moment. After it the game is open-ended: the best farm, every recipe,
every animal at 10 hearts.

## Order to build

1. **Energy and a day plan**: an energy bar from tool use, refilled by sleep and food.
2. **Seasons and seed lists that change with them**, plus Wheat and Corn.
3. **Gold sinks**: the horse purchase, the field deed, and the coop and barn.
4. **Animals**: buy, pet for hearts, feed hay, breed.
5. **Smith and bars**: furnace, tool upgrades, sprinklers.
6. **Mine floors** and the lift.
7. **The ledger and its rewards.**
8. **House upgrades and furniture.**
